APEX CUSTOMS — TOP-DOWN VEHICLE IMAGE PROMPTS (for ChatGPT / GPT-4o image generation)
=====================================================================================

HOW TO USE
- Paste a prompt into ChatGPT. When it asks (or in the size picker) choose LANDSCAPE / WIDE (3:2).
- Each prompt below is self-contained — you can paste any one on its own.
- For a perfectly MATCHING set, do them in ONE chat: paste #1, then for the rest say:
  "Same exact style, lighting, overhead angle, scale and dark background as the previous
   image — change only the vehicle to: <paste the vehicle line>."
- Download each, rename to: sedan.png, hatchback.png, suv.png, coupe.png, doublecab.png, singlecab.png
- Send them back to me and I'll drop them in and line the hotspots up.

MUST-HAVES (already baked into every prompt — don't remove):
- True top-down, directly overhead, orthographic (no perspective/tilt)
- Front of vehicle points to TOP of frame; vehicle centered, ~70% of width
- Roof removed / cutaway so the interior (seats, doors, dash, boot) is visible
- No text, labels, logos, watermarks, people or measurement lines
